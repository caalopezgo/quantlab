## Longer

Quant Lab is a small research system for stating an investing rule, measuring it on history, applying risk constraints, and practicing with paper trades. Strategies emit intent; a risk engine can resize them; a paper broker executes only after that review. The backtester uses next-session opens so same-day look-ahead is not available.

More detail: [architecture](ARCHITECTURE.md) · [sample study](CASE_NOTE.md)
